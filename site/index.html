Check these directly

These big programs aren't in the automatic feed, because their sites can't be read automatically or need an account. Check them yourself every few weeks:

  • Lockheed Martin careers: search "intern". Their site blocks automated readers, so only postings that show up on the community lists appear above.
  • NASA internships (OSTEM): NASA's own paid internships at every center.
  • USAJOBS: student / Pathways jobs: federal internships (NASA, Air Force, Navy, Army labs) that can turn into full-time jobs.
  • ORISE / Zintellect: research internships at DOE and DoD labs, including ORNL programs.
  • DoD SMART Scholarship: tuition plus paid summer internships at DoD labs, with a job afterward.
Where this data comes from

Each week the tracker reads company career sites directly (descriptions come from the company), plus community internship lists for everything else. Postings disappear when the company takes them down. Match scores are keyword-based, so treat them as a sorting aid and read the posting.
